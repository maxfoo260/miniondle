    How to play

    A new mystery Minion appears every day. Study the photo at the top and figure out which named Minion it is.

    • You get 5 guesses. Start typing a name and pick from the list.
    • Each guess reveals how it compares to the answer across Eyes, Height, Hair and Debut.
    • Green means that trait matches the mystery Minion.
    • Grey means it doesn't match. Height shows ⬆ / ⬇ arrows toward the answer.

    Solve it in as few guesses and as little time as possible to climb the daily leaderboard!
