    How to play

    A new mystery Minion appears every day. Study the photo at the top and figure out which named Minion it is.

    • You get 5 guesses. Start typing a name and pick from the list.
    • Each guess reveals how it compares to the answer across Eyes, Height and Hair.
    • Green means that trait matches the mystery Minion.
    • Grey means it doesn't match.

    Solve it in as few guesses and as little time as possible to climb the daily leaderboard!

    Two modes: Daily is one shared puzzle per day with the leaderboard. Unlimited gives you a fresh random Minion every time — play as much as you want. When you win, you're told which film the Minion debuted in.
